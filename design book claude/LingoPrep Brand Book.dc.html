01 — Brand strategy

Free, honest test preparation

LingoPrep exists because credible IELTS and TOEFL practice is priced out of reach for most candidates. The brand's job is to look and feel as trustworthy as a paid test-preparation service while being free, transparent about its AI scoring, and clear that it is independent of the examination boards.

Purpose

Give every candidate a realistic, instantly-scored practice environment for all four language skills — at no cost.

Positioning

The free AI examiner: exam-accurate practice with band-level feedback, available the moment a candidate decides to prepare.

Primary audience

18–30 year-old university applicants and skilled-migration candidates in South Asia, preparing largely unsupervised on a laptop or phone.

Personality

Calm examiner, not cheerleader. Precise, encouraging, quietly confident. Never gimmicky, never alarmist about scores.

Four brand principles

Exam-true

Timers, layouts and question types mirror the real test. No invented formats.

Legible first

Reading passages set the pace of the design. Decoration never competes with text.

Explain the score

Every band comes with criteria, an explanation and a next action.

Free without asterisks

No locked modules, no countdown offers, no fake scarcity anywhere in the product.

02 — Voice & tone

Write like a fair examiner

Do write
  • “Band 6.5 — your argument is clear, but paragraph three repeats the introduction.”
  • “Each recording plays once, exactly as in the real test.”
  • “Write at least 250 words. You have 40 minutes.”
  • “Free. All four modules, no account required to practise.”
Don't write
  • “Amazing! You're a superstar writer! 🎉”
  • “Guaranteed Band 8 in 7 days.”
  • “Oops! Something went wrong :(”
  • “Unlock premium to see your real score.”
Context Tone Example

Inside a testNeutral, instructionalChoose the correct letter A, B, C or D.

ErrorsResponsible, actionableWe couldn't save your essay. Your draft is still here — try again.
Empty statesInviting, one actionNo results yet. Take a reading test to start your record.

Naming rules. Always “LingoPrep” — one word, capital L, capital P, never “Lingo Prep”, “LINGOPREP” in body copy, or “lingoprep” outside a URL. Exam names are always styled “IELTS”, “TOEFL iBT®”. Say “practice test”, never “mock exam”. Say “band score” for IELTS and “section score” for TOEFL.

03 — Primary logo

The Progress Bubble

A speech bubble — language, spoken and written — containing three ascending bars: measurable progress toward a target band. The tail doubles as a downward pointer, reading as a cursor placing the learner inside the conversation. This is the recommended primary mark.

LingoPrep
Primary horizontal lockup — default for web headers, documents and slides
Construction

The mark is built on an 8×8 unit grid. Bubble stroke = 0.7u, corner radius = 2.5u, bars are 1u wide with 1u gaps and rise in 2u steps. The wordmark's cap height equals 4u; the gap between mark and wordmark equals the mark's stroke ×3.

04 — Logo suggestions

Five directions

Each direction is a complete, original mark that works at 16px and on a certificate. A is recommended; B is the closest in spirit to the multi-coloured cluster in the reference; C is the safest at tiny sizes.

A
Progress Bubble
Recommended

Language plus measurable improvement in one shape. Single colour, so it prints, embroiders and favicons cleanly.

B
Four Skills Pinwheel

Four rounded bars — Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking — turning around a centre. Closest to the reference's coloured-cluster energy while being geometrically distinct.

C
LP Monogram Tile

A squared badge holding an interlocked L and P. The most robust option for app icons, avatars and 16px favicons.

D
Answer Bubble

A solid bubble carrying a correction tick: the moment of getting it right. Highest contrast of the set — strong on dark UI and social avatars.

E
Meridian Book

An open book whose pages curve like meridians — study and international mobility. The most academic of the five; weakest below 24px.

How to choose
  • Pick A if the platform's promise is measurable improvement.
  • Pick B if the four modules are the headline of the product.
  • Pick C if the identity will live mostly as an app icon.
  • Pick D if you want maximum recognisability on dark backgrounds.
  • Pick E if the audience skews institutional or academic.

Whichever is chosen, keep one mark only. Do not ship two marks in parallel.

08 — Typography

Proxima Nova, with JetBrains Mono for figures

One family carries the whole brand. Proxima Nova Bold (700) sets every heading, wordmark, button and label; Proxima Nova Medium (500) sets all body copy, passages and form text. Two weights only — no light, no black, no italics. JetBrains Mono is reserved for timers, band scores and question numbers.

11 — Imagery, motion & applications

Real candidates, honest moments

Hero photograph — 16:10, place your asset here
Photography direction

Natural light, shallow depth of field, one or two people, mid-action rather than posed. Candidates studying, arriving, walking with a laptop — never stock-smiling into camera, never handshakes, never flags. Warm neutral grade; crimson appears in the frame as an accessory or garment, not as a filter. Crop to 16:10 for heroes, 4:3 for cards, 1:1 for avatars. Always leave a quiet area for the overlay card.

Illustration & graphics

No mascots, no 3D blobs, no gradient meshes. Where a diagram is needed, build it from the icon vocabulary: 1.8px strokes, accent for the single element in focus, neutral for everything else.

14 — Legal & assets

Independence, stated plainly

Required footer disclaimer — every page

LingoPrep is an independent learning platform. IELTS® is a registered trademark of the University of Cambridge ESOL Examinations, the British Council and IDP Education Australia. TOEFL® and TOEFL iBT® are registered trademarks of Educational Testing Service (ETS). This platform is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or approved by any of these trademark owners. Practice scores are AI-generated estimates and are not official results.

Never do
  • Use the IELTS, IDP, British Council, ETS or TOEFL logos anywhere.
  • Imitate an examination board's logo, colours-plus-wordmark combination or typography.
  • Say “official”, “certified”, “accredited” or “partner”.
  • Present a practice band as a result usable for admissions or visas.
  • Register a domain or handle containing “ielts” or “toefl”.
Always do
  • Use exam names as plain descriptive words in text only, with ® on first use.
  • Label every score “practice estimate”.
  • Keep the disclaimer visible without scrolling on score pages.
  • State that scoring is AI-generated wherever a band is shown.
  • Keep LingoPrep's own mark as the only logo on the page.
